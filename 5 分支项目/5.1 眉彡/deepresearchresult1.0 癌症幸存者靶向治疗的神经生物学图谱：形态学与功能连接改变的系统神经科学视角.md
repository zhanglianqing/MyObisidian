# Neurobiological Mapping of Targeted Therapy in Cancer Survivorship: A Systems Neuroscience Perspective on Morphometric and Functional Connectivity Alterations

# 癌症幸存者靶向治疗的神经生物学图谱：形态学与功能连接改变的系统神经科学视角

The transformative success of oncology over the past two decades has shifted the clinical focus from acute survival to the management of long-term treatment sequelae. As precision medicine becomes the standard of care, the neurotoxic profile of targeted therapies—including tyrosine kinase inhibitors (TKIs), monoclonal antibodies, and immune checkpoint inhibitors (ICIs)—has emerged as a critical determinant of quality of life for cancer survivors. The phenomenon of cancer-related cognitive impairment (CRCI) is no longer viewed as a transient side effect but as a complex neurobiological state characterized by identifiable structural and functional reorganization of the central nervous system. Advanced neuroimaging modalities, particularly Voxel-Based Morphometry (VBM) and resting-state functional Magnetic Resonance Imaging (rs-fMRI), provide the necessary resolution to map these changes with high precision.[^1]

在过去的二十年里，肿瘤学的变革性成功使临床重点从急性期生存转向了长期治疗后遗症的管理。随着精准医疗成为标准护理，靶向药物（包括酪氨酸激酶抑制剂 TKIs、单克隆抗体和免疫检查点抑制剂 ICIs）的神经毒性特征已成为决定癌症幸存者生活质量的关键因素。癌症相关认知障碍（CRCI）不再被视为暂时的副作用，而是一个复杂的神经生物学状态，其特征是中枢神经系统可识别的结构和功能重组。先进的神经影像学方法，特别是基于体素的形态学测量（VBM）和静息态功能磁共振成像（rs-fMRI），为高精度描绘这些变化提供了必要的分辨率。[^1]

The biological underpinnings of CRCI in the context of targeted therapy involve a multifaceted interplay between the direct neurotoxic effects of systemic agents and the secondary inflammatory responses triggered by the malignancy itself. While traditional chemotherapy was characterized by non-specific cytotoxic damage, targeted therapies often interfere with signaling pathways—such as the Epidermal Growth Factor Receptor (EGFR) and Vascular Endothelial Growth Factor (VEGF) pathways—that are essential for neuronal survival, synaptic plasticity, and vascular homeostasis.[^3] The resulting neural alterations manifest as regional gray matter atrophy, microstructural white matter degradation, and the disruption of large-scale functional networks such as the Default Mode Network (DMN) and the Salience Network.[^5]

在靶向治疗背景下，CRCI 的生物学基础涉及系统性药物的直接神经毒性作用与恶性肿瘤本身引发的继发性炎症反应之间多维度的相互作用。虽然传统化疗的特点是非特异性细胞毒性损伤，但靶向治疗通常会干扰对于神经元存活、突触可塑性和血管稳态至关重要的信号通路——如表皮生长因子受体（EGFR）和血管内皮生长因子（VEGF）通路。[^3]由此产生的神经改变表现为区域性灰质萎缩、微结构白质退化，以及大规模功能网络（如默认模式网络 DMN 和显著网络）的破坏。[^5]

## Macrostructural Reorganization via Voxel-Based Morphometry

## 通过基于体素的形态学测量观察宏观结构重组

Voxel-Based Morphometry (VBM) has established a robust framework for quantifying regional changes in brain volume and density. By performing voxel-wise comparisons across the entire brain, researchers have identified consistent patterns of gray matter (GM) loss in survivors of various cancers treated with targeted and systemic therapies. These structural changes often target the frontal and temporal lobes, regions that serve as the neuroanatomical substrate for executive function, memory, and emotional regulation.[^6]

基于体素的形态学测量（VBM）为量化脑容量和密度的区域性变化建立了一个强大的框架。通过在全脑范围内进行体素级比较，研究人员在接受靶向治疗和系统治疗的各种癌症幸存者中发现了灰质（GM）流失的一致模式。这些结构变化通常针对额叶和颞叶，这些区域是执行功能、记忆和情绪调节的神经解剖基础。[^6]

### Regional Atrophy and the Cingulo-Opercular Infrastructure

### 区域性萎缩与扣带-岛盖结构

One of the most replicated findings in the VBM literature is the significant reduction in gray matter volume within the insula and the inferior frontal gyrus.[^6] The insula is a primary hub of the cingulo-opercular network (CON), which is responsible for task control, interoceptive awareness, and the integration of emotional and sensory information. In breast cancer survivors treated with chemotherapy and targeted agents, consistent volume reductions in the left insula have been linked to symptoms of fatigue and diminished task-switching abilities.[^6] The vulnerability of the insula may be related to its high metabolic activity and its central role in processing the systemic inflammatory signals—such as C-reactive protein (CRP) and pro-inflammatory cytokines—that are frequently elevated during targeted treatment.[^4]

VBM 文献中最常被验证的发现之一是岛叶和额下回灰质体积的显著减少。[^6] 岛叶是扣带-岛盖网络（CON）的主要核心，该网络负责任务控制、内感认知以及情绪和感觉信息的整合。在接受化疗和靶向药物治疗的乳腺癌幸存者中，左侧岛叶体积的一致减少与疲劳症状和任务切换能力下降有关。[^6] 岛叶的脆弱性可能与其高代谢活性以及在处理系统性炎症信号（如 C 反应蛋白 CRP 和促炎细胞因子）中的核心作用有关，这些信号在靶向治疗期间经常升高。[^4]

Reductions in the dorsolateral prefrontal cortex (dlPFC) and the anterior cingulate cortex (ACC) further corroborate the "executive dysfunction" profile of CRCI. VBM studies have demonstrated that these reductions occur acutely after the initiation of treatment and may persist long after its completion.[^9] For instance, in patients undergoing neoadjuvant chemotherapy combined with targeted agents, widespread morphological alterations in the bilateral superior frontal gyrus were observed within just one cycle of treatment.[^11] These early structural markers suggest that the brain's "structural reserve" is taxed almost immediately upon exposure to neurotoxic stressors.

背外侧前额叶皮层（dlPFC）和前扣带回皮层（ACC）的减少进一步证实了 CRCI 的“执行功能障碍”特征。VBM 研究表明，这些减少在治疗开始后急性发生，并可能在治疗结束后长期存在。[^9] 例如，在接受新辅助化疗联合靶向药物的患者中，仅一个治疗周期后就观察到双侧额上回广泛的形态学改变。[^11] 这些早期结构标志物表明，大脑的“结构储备”在暴露于神经毒性压力源后几乎立即受到损耗。

### Subcortical and Temporal Lobe Sensitivity

### 皮层下及颞叶敏感性

The parahippocampal gyrus and the hippocampus itself exhibit unique trajectories of change in response to targeted therapy. While many cortical regions show atrophy, some longitudinal studies have noted transient increases in hippocampal volume following the first cycle of therapy, followed by progressive decline.[^10] This biphasic response may reflect an initial neuroinflammatory swelling or a maladaptive compensatory effort before the onset of permanent neurotoxic damage. The long-term atrophy of the parahippocampal gyrus is significantly associated with deficits in episodic memory and spatial navigation, common complaints among survivors.[^6]

旁海马回和海马体本身在应对靶向治疗时表现出独特的变化轨迹。虽然许多皮层区域表现出萎缩，但一些纵向研究指出，在第一个治疗周期后海马体积会出现短暂增加，随后逐进下降。[^10] 这种双相反应可能反映了在永久性神经毒性损伤发生之前的初始神经炎症肿胀或失调的代偿努力。旁海马回的长期萎缩与情景记忆和空间导航能力的缺陷显著相关，这是幸存者常见的抱怨。[^6]

In patients treated with EGFR-TKIs for non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC), long-term therapy has been associated with subtle yet statistically significant reductions in gray matter volume.[^14] A retrospective study of 21 patients utilizing high-resolution T1-weighted imaging revealed that even in the absence of overt brain metastases, the prolonged inhibition of the EGFR pathway—which is involved in glial and neuronal maintenance—results in a measurable decrease in global and regional brain volume.[^14] This suggests that "successful" treatment with targeted agents may carry a hidden cost to structural brain integrity that necessitates long-term neuroimaging surveillance.

在接受 EGFR-TKIs 治疗的非小细胞肺癌（NSCLC）患者中，长期治疗与细微但具有统计学意义的灰质体积减少有关。[^14] 一项利用高分辨率 T1 加权成像对 21 名患者进行的回顾性研究显示，即使在没有明显脑转移的情况下，长期抑制 EGFR 通路（涉及胶质细胞和神经元的维护）也会导致全脑和区域脑容量的可测量下降。[^14] 这表明使用靶向药物的“成功”治疗可能会对大脑结构的完整性带来潜在代价，因此需要长期的神经影像学监测。

## Functional Connectomics and Resting-State Dynamics

## 功能连接组学与静息态动力学

Resting-state functional MRI (rs-fMRI) has shifted the focus from static anatomy to the dynamic communication between brain regions. By analyzing spontaneous BOLD signal fluctuations, researchers have identified that targeted therapies disrupt the "small-world" architecture of the brain, leading to global inefficiency and network fragmentation.[^5]

静息态功能磁共振成像（rs-fMRI）将研究重点从静态解剖转向了脑区之间的动态通信。通过分析自发的血氧水平依赖（BOLD）信号波动，研究人员发现靶向治疗破坏了大脑的“小世界”架构，导致全局效率低下和网络碎片化。[^5]

### The Default Mode Network and Accelerated Aging

### 默认模式网络与加速衰老

The Default Mode Network (DMN) is the most critical functional marker of cognitive health in the oncology population. Consisting of the medial prefrontal cortex (mPFC), the posterior cingulate cortex (PCC)/precuneus, and the angular gyrus, the DMN is essential for self-referential thought and internal memory retrieval. Targeted treatments have been shown to specifically decouple the DMN, particularly the link between the anterior and posterior nodes.[^5]

默认模式网络（DMN）是肿瘤人群认知健康最关键的功能标志物。DMN 由内侧前额叶皮层（mPFC）、后扣带回皮层（PCC）/前楔叶和角回组成，对于自我参照思维和内部记忆提取至关重要。靶向治疗已被证明会专门使 DMN 解耦，特别是前部和后部节点之间的连接。[^5]

In older survivors, this decoupling takes the form of a "posterior-to-anterior shift" in connectivity strength. Research indicates that after systemic treatment, survivors exhibit weaker connectivity in the posterior DMN nodes (such as the precuneus and retrosplenial cortex) and a compensatory increase in connectivity within the anterior nodes (mPFC).[^18] This pattern is highly characteristic of accelerated biological aging, suggesting that cancer treatment essentially "ages" the functional connectivity of the brain by several years.[^3] The magnitude of this DMN disruption has been found to correlate with objective measures of verbal memory and processing speed.[^5]

在老年幸存者中，这种解耦表现为连接强度的“由后向前偏移”。研究表明，在接受系统治疗后，幸存者的 DMN 后部节点（如前楔叶和后扣带皮层）连接减弱，而前部节点（mPFC）的连接出现代偿性增加。[^18] 这种模式是生物学加速衰老的高度典型特征，表明癌症治疗实质上使大脑的功能连接“老化”了几年。[^3] DMN 破坏的程度已被发现与言语记忆和处理速度的客观测量指标相关。[^5]

### Topological Properties and Network Efficiency

### 拓扑属性与网络效率

Using graph theory, researchers have quantified the brain's ability to process information efficiently. Healthy brains typically exhibit a balance of high local clustering and short path lengths. However, in cancer survivors, the functional connectome often shifts toward a more "regular" or less complex network topology.[^16]

利用图论，研究人员量化了大脑高效处理信息的能力。健康的大脑通常表现出高局部聚类和短路径长度的平衡。然而，在癌症幸存者中，功能连接组通常向更“规则”或复杂性更低的网络拓扑结构转变。[^16]

1. **Global Efficiency**: Many studies report a significant reduction in global efficiency post-treatment, meaning that the brain must expend more "neural energy" to transfer information across distant regions.[^5]
1. **Path Length**: An increase in characteristic path length indicates a loss of the direct long-range connections that are vital for high-order cognitive processes like executive function and complex problem solving.[^17]
1. **Modular Disruption**: The modularity of the brain—its organization into specialized communities—is often reduced, leading to "noisy" communication and decreased modular segregation.[^16]
1. **全局效率**：许多研究报告治疗后全局效率显著降低，这意味着大脑必须消耗更多的“神经能量”来跨远端区域传输信息。[^5]
1. **路径长度**：特征路径长度的增加表明失去了对于执行功能和复杂问题解决等高阶认知过程至关重要的直接远距离连接。[^17]
1. **模块化破坏**：大脑的模块化——即组织成专门社群的结构——通常会减少，导致通信“嘈杂”和模块隔离度降低。[^16]

## Targeted Therapeutics: Specific Agent-Related Neurotoxicity

## 靶向药物：特定药物相关的神经毒性

The shift toward targeted therapy has introduced specific agents with unique neuroimaging profiles. Understanding these differences is essential for developing personalized prevention and rehabilitation strategies.

向靶向治疗的转变引入了具有独特神经影像学特征的特定药物。了解这些差异对于制定个性化的预防和康复策略至关重要。

### Tyrosine Kinase Inhibitors (TKIs) and Vascular Neurotoxicity

### 烙氨酸激酶抑制剂 (TKIs) 与血管神经毒性

Tyrosine kinase inhibitors, particularly those targeting the VEGF pathway (e.g., Sunitinib, Sorafenib, Tivozanib), impact the brain's microenvironment by altering vascular permeability and blood flow.[^24] While these agents are intended to starve the tumor of its blood supply, they also affect the healthy cerebral vasculature. Neuroimaging studies of patients receiving tivozanib have shown significant decreases in tumor vascular permeability and blood flow, which correlates with changes in global functional connectivity.[^24]

烙氨酸激酶抑制剂，特别是针对 VEGF 通路的药物（如舒尼替尼、索拉非尼、替沃扎尼），通过改变血管通透性和血流量来影响大脑的微环境。[^24] 虽然这些药物旨在切断肿瘤的血液供应，但它们也会影响健康的脑血管系统。接受替沃扎尼治疗的患者神经影像学研究显示，肿瘤血管通透性和血流量显著下降，这与全局功能连接的变化相关。[^24]

Furthermore, VEGFR-TKIs have been associated with Posterior Reversible Encephalopathy Syndrome (PRES), a condition characterized by vasogenic edema in the posterior parietal and occipital lobes.[^25] While PRES is often reversible, it highlights the potential for targeted agents to cause acute, severe disruptions in the blood-brain barrier (BBB). Long-term exposure to these agents can lead to a state of chronic hypoperfusion, which may contribute to the progressive gray matter atrophy and white matter hyperintensities observed in VBM analysis.[^14]

此外，VEGFR-TKIs 与后部可逆性脑病综合征（PRES）有关，这是一种以后顶叶和枕叶血管源性水肿为特征的疾病。[^25] 虽然 PRES 通常是可逆的，但它突显了靶向药物导致血脑屏障（BBB）急性严重破坏的可能性。长期暴露于这些药物可能导致慢性低灌注状态，这可能促进了 VBM 分析中观察到的逐进性灰质萎缩和白质高信号。[^14]

### Immune Checkpoint Inhibitors (ICIs) and Neuroinflammation

### 免疫检查点抑制剂 (ICIs) 与神经炎症

Immunotherapy has introduced a new class of neurotoxic effects collectively known as neurological immune-related adverse events (N-irAEs). Immune checkpoint inhibitors (ICIs) like Nivolumab (anti-PD-1) and Ipilimumab (anti-CTLA-4) work by removing the "brakes" on the immune system, which can lead to off-target autoimmune attacks on the nervous system.[^27]

免疫治疗引入了一类新的神经毒性效应，统称为神经系统免疫相关不良事件（N-irAEs）。免疫检查点抑制剂（ICIs），如纳武利尤单抗（抗 PD-1）和伊匹木单抗（抗 CTLA-4），通过解除免疫系统的“制动”起作用，这可能导致对神经系统的脱靶自身免疫攻击。[^27]

- **Encephalitis**: The most common ICI-related CNS toxicity, which may manifest as T2/FLAIR hyperintensities in the limbic system or temporal lobes, similar to autoimmune encephalitis.[^28]
- **Hypophysitis**: A hallmark of ICI therapy, particularly with CTLA-4 inhibitors. Neuroimaging reveals a transient enlargement of the pituitary gland (often ≥7 mm) followed by secondary atrophy and hormonal insufficiency.[^30]
- **Blood-Brain Barrier Alterations**: ICIs can increase the permeability of the BBB through the systemic release of cytokines such as IL-6 and TNF-α. This disruption allows for greater infiltration of activated T-cells and other inflammatory mediators into the brain parenchyma, potentially exacerbating the "chemobrain" phenotype.[^3]
- **脑炎**：最常见的 ICI 相关中枢神经系统毒性，可能表现为边缘系统或颞叶的 T2/FLAIR 高信号，类似于自身免疫性脑炎。[^28]
- **垂体炎**：ICI 治疗的标志，特别是在使用 CTLA-4 抑制剂时。神经影像学显示垂体腺出现短暂增大（通常 ≥7 mm），随后出现继发性萎缩和激素分泌不足。[^30]
- **血脑屏障改变**：ICIs 可通过系统性释放细胞因子（如 IL-6 和 TNF-α）增加 BBB 的通透性。这种破坏允许激活的 T 细胞和其他炎症介质更多地浸润到脑实质中，可能加剧“化疗脑”表型。[^3]

Recent research suggests that survivors treated with a combination of chemotherapy and ICIs (chemoICI) may experience different cognitive trajectories than those treated with chemotherapy alone.[^29] The added inflammatory burden of immunotherapy may result in more persistent deficits in memory and executive function, necessitating specific monitoring of functional connectivity markers in the DMN and salience networks.

最近的研究表明，接受化疗联合 ICIs（化疗-免疫联合治疗）的幸存者可能比仅接受化疗的患者经历不同的认知轨迹。[^29] 免疫治疗增加的炎症负担可能导致更持久的记忆和执行功能缺陷，因此有必要专门监测 DMN 和显著网络中的功能连接标志物。

## Mechanisms of Neurotoxicity and the Accelerated Aging Model

## 神经毒性机制与加速衰老模型

The convergence of VBM and rs-fMRI findings supports the hypothesis that cancer treatment induces a state of accelerated biological aging in the brain. This model suggests that the physiological stressors of therapy—including oxidative stress, neuroinflammation, and mitochondrial dysfunction—prematurely exhaust the brain's homeostatic mechanisms.[^3]

VBM 和 rs-fMRI 研究结果的趋同支持了这样一种假设：癌症治疗诱发了大脑生物学加速衰老的状态。该模型认为，治疗带来的生理压力源——包括氧化应激、神经炎症和线粒体功能障碍——过早地耗尽了大脑的稳态平衡机制。[^3]

### Oxidative Stress and Synaptic Integrity

### 氧化应激与突触完整性

Many targeted therapies and chemotherapeutic agents generate reactive oxygen species (ROS) that overwhelm the brain's antioxidant defenses. This oxidative injury leads to lipid peroxidation, protein oxidation, and DNA damage within neurons and glia.[^3] At the macroscopic level, this is observed as a reduction in cortical thickness and gray matter density. Furthermore, oxidative stress disrupts synaptic plasticity, the fundamental process by which the brain learns and forms memories. This disruption is reflected in rs-fMRI as reduced regional homogeneity (ReHo) and weakened functional connectivity within memory-related networks.[^22]

许多靶向治疗和化疗药物会产生过量的活性氧（ROS），使大脑的抗氧化防御系统崩溃。这种氧化损伤导致神经元和胶质细胞内的脂质过氧化、蛋白质氧化和 DNA 损伤。[^3] 在宏观层面，这表现为皮层厚度和灰质密度的降低。此外，氧化应激破坏了突触可塑性，即大脑学习和形成记忆的基础过程。这种破坏在 rs-fMRI 中体现为局部一致性（ReHo）降低以及记忆相关网络内功能连接的减弱。[^22]

### The Blood-Brain Barrier as a Regulatory Hub

### 作为调节中枢的血脑屏障

The integrity of the blood-brain barrier is central to the prevention of treatment-related neurotoxicity. Targeted agents can compromise the BBB either through direct endothelial toxicity or indirectly through systemic inflammation.[^3] A disrupted BBB allows for the entry of peripheral cytokines and neurotoxic metabolites, which activate microglia and astrocytes, initiating a cascade of neuroinflammation.[^3] Recent studies utilizing Dynamic Contrast-Enhanced (DCE) MRI have shown that abnormal BBB permeability in the white matter is significantly associated with cognitive impairment and cancer-related pain in lung cancer patients, providing a critical imaging-based link between vascular dysfunction and neurological symptoms.[^32]

血脑屏障的完整性是预防治疗相关神经毒性的核心。靶向药物既可以通过直接的内皮毒性，也可以通过系统性炎症间接破坏 BBB。[^3] 受损的 BBB 允许外周细胞因子和神经毒性代谢物进入，激活小胶质细胞和星形胶质细胞，引发一系列神经炎症反应。[^3] 最近利用动态对比增强（DCE）MRI 进行的研究显示，肺癌患者白质中异常的 BBB 通透性与认知障碍和癌症相关疼痛显著相关，这为血管功能障碍与神经症状之间提供了关键的影像学证据。[^32]

### Epigenetic Remodeling and Cellular Senescence

### 表观遗传重组与细胞衰老

Treatment-induced cellular senescence—where cells cease to divide but remain metabolically active—contributes to the "aging" phenotype of the survivor's brain. Senescent cells secrete a cocktail of pro-inflammatory factors (SASP) that damage neighboring healthy cells.[^3] This process, combined with epigenetic remodeling (such as changes in DNA methylation), can lead to long-term alterations in gene expression that maintain the brain in a state of chronic inflammation and reduced plasticity. Neuroimaging metrics such as "brain age" prediction algorithms integrate these multi-level damages into a single metric that can be used for early triage and intervention.[^19]

治疗诱导的细胞衰老（即细胞停止分裂但保持代谢活跃）促进了幸存者大脑的“衰老”表型。衰老细胞分泌一组促炎因子（SASP），损害相邻的健康细胞。[^3] 这一过程结合表观遗传重组（如 DNA 甲基化的改变），可能导致基因表达的长期改变，使大脑维持在慢性炎症和塑性降低的状态。诸如“脑龄”预测算法等神经影像指标将这些多维损伤整合为一个单一指标，可用于早期分诊和干预。[^19]

## Clinical Implications: Connecting Brain Changes to Cognitive Profiles

## 临床意义：将大脑变化与认知特征联系起来

The ultimate goal of mapping neuroimaging changes is to bridge the gap between biological alterations and the lived experience of the cancer survivor. The deficits observed in memory, attention, and executive function are not random but correspond to the specific structural and functional hubs targeted by treatment.

描绘神经影像学变化的最终目标是弥合生物学改变与癌症幸存者现实体验之间的鸿沟。记忆、注意力和执行功能方面的缺陷并非偶然，而是对应于受治疗影响的特定结构和功能中枢。

### Memory and the Parahippocampal-Precuneus Axis

### 记忆与旁海马-前楔叶轴

Memory complaints are perhaps the most common symptom of CRCI. VBM studies have consistently identified volume loss in the parahippocampal gyrus and the precuneus, both of which are central to episodic memory and self-referential processing.[^6] In breast cancer survivors, reduced gray matter volume in the left precuneus has been specifically linked to poorer verbal memory performance.[^20] Functional connectivity analyses further support this, showing that reduced coupling between the precuneus and the frontal lobes is a hallmark of treatment-induced memory decline.[^20]

记忆力抱怨或许是 CRCI 最常见的症状。VBM 研究一致发现了旁海马回和前楔叶的体积减少，这两个区域都是情景记忆和自我参照处理的核心。[^6] 在乳腺癌幸存者中，左侧前楔叶灰质体积的减少与言语记忆表现较差特异性相关。[^20] 功能连接分析进一步支持了这一点，显示前楔叶与额叶之间的耦合减弱是治疗诱导的记忆力下降的标志。[^20]

### Attention and the Dorsal Attention Network

### 注意力与背侧注意网络

Impairments in attention and processing speed are often linked to white matter integrity and the connectivity of the Dorsal Attention Network (DAN). DTI studies have shown reduced fractional anisotropy (FA) in white matter tracts such as the superior longitudinal fasciculus, which connects the frontal and parietal regions.[^6] This microstructural damage results in slower signal transmission, explaining why patients often describe their thinking as "cloudy" or "slow." rs-fMRI has also revealed decreased connectivity within the DAN, which correlates with longer reaction times and difficulties in sustained attention.[^6]

注意力和处理速度的受损通常与白质完整性以及背侧注意网络（DAN）的连接有关。DTI 研究表明，额顶叶之间的白质纤维束（如上纵束）的分数各向异性（FA）降低。[^6] 这种微结构损伤导致信号传输变慢，解释了为什么患者经常将他们的思维描述为“浑浊”或“迟钝”。rs-fMRI 还揭示了 DAN 内部连接的降低，这与更长的反应时间和持续注意力困难相关。[^6]

### Executive Function and the Frontal-Subcortical Circuits

### 执行功能与额叶-皮层下回路

Executive functions—including planning, task-switching, and impulse control—are mediated by complex circuits involving the prefrontal cortex and the basal ganglia. VBM has revealed significant atrophy in the dlPFC and the caudate nucleus in survivors treated with systemic therapy.[^7] These structural changes are mirrored in functional connectivity as weakened coupling between the mPFC and the rest of the executive control network. Interestingly, some survivors show *increased* activation in these regions during task performance, which is interpreted as a compensatory effort to maintain performance despite a damaged neural substrate.[^20]

执行功能——包括规划、任务切换和冲动控制——由涉及前额叶皮层和基底节的复杂回路调节。VBM 显示，接受系统治疗的幸存者在 dlPFC 和尾状核中存在显著萎缩。[^7] 这些结构变化在功能连接上表现为 mPFC 与执行控制网络其余部分之间的耦合减弱。有趣的是，一些幸存者在执行任务期间表现出这些区域的激活*增加*，这被解释为在神经底质受损的情况下为维持表现而进行的代偿性努力。[^20]

## Longitudinal Trajectories and the Potential for Recovery

## 纵向轨迹与恢复潜力

A critical question for survivors is whether the brain can recover from treatment-related neurotoxicity. Longitudinal studies have provided a mixed but nuanced picture of the brain's resilience.

对于幸存者来说，一个关键问题是大脑能否从治疗相关的神经毒性中恢复。纵向研究描绘了一幅复杂但细致的大脑韧性图景。

1. **Acute Response**: Widespread structural and functional changes are observed within weeks to months of treatment initiation. This includes rapid gray matter loss and the fragmentation of functional networks.[^10]
1. **Early Delayed Period (1-6 months)**: This period often shows the peak of neuroimaging abnormalities. Many of the functional disruptions observed here—such as decreased DMN connectivity—are predictive of long-term cognitive outcomes.[^5]
1. **Late Delayed Period (>6 months)**: While some survivors show evidence of partial structural recovery at the one-year mark (particularly in the frontal regions), others exhibit persistent or even progressive decline.[^1] Persistent volume loss in the temporal lobes and cerebellum suggests that some areas may be less resilient to the initial toxic insult.[^12]
1. **急性反应**：在治疗开始后的数周至数月内可观察到广泛的结构和功能变化。这包括迅速的灰质流失和功能网络的碎片化。[^10]
1. **早期延迟期（1-6 个月）**：这一时期通常是神经影像异常的高峰期。在此观察到的许多功能中断（如 DMN 连接降低）对长期认知结局具有预测性。[^5]
1. **晚期延迟期（>6 个月）**：虽然一些幸存者在一年左右显示出部分结构恢复的迹象（特别是在额叶区域），但其他人则表现出持续甚至逐渐加重的退化。[^1] 颞叶和小脑持续的体积流失表明，某些区域对最初的毒性打击可能缺乏韧性。[^12]

Cognitive reserve appears to be a major factor in the recovery process. Survivors with higher levels of education or pre-treatment cognitive stimulation may be more "resilient" to neurotoxic damage, showing a greater ability to normalize their functional connectivity and "brain age" metrics during the recovery phase.[^19]

认知储备似乎是恢复过程中的一个主要因素。受教育程度较高或治疗前认知刺激较多的幸存者可能对神经毒性损伤更具“韧性”，在恢复阶段表现出更强的使功能连接和“脑龄”指标恢复正常的能力。[^19]

## Future Directions: Precision Neuro-Oncology

## 未来方向：精准神经肿瘤学

As the field of neuro-oncology advances, several emerging trends will shape the future of neuroimaging in cancer survivors.

随着神经肿瘤学领域的发展，几个新兴趋势将重塑癌症幸存者神经影像学的未来。

### Radiomics and Multi-Modal Integration

### 影像组学与多模态整合

The integration of radiomics—the high-throughput extraction of quantitative data from standard medical images—with VBM and rs-fMRI offers the potential for even more sensitive biomarkers. Radiomic features can detect subtle changes in tissue texture and heterogeneity that precede gross volume loss.[^13] Combining these features with blood-based biomarkers (such as CRP or neurofilament light chain) will enable clinicians to develop high-precision models for predicting which patients are most at risk for severe CRCI.[^4]

将影像组学（从标准医学图像中高通量提取定量数据）与 VBM 和 rs-fMRI 相结合，有望提供更灵敏的生物标志物。影像组学特征可以检测到在出现明显体积损失之前的组织纹理和异质性的细微变化。[^13] 将这些特征与血液生物标志物（如 CRP 或神经丝轻链）结合，将使临床医生能够开发高精度模型，预测哪些患者面临严重的 CRCI 风险。[^4]

### Targeted Rehabilitation and Neuromodulation

### 靶向康复与神经调控

Understanding the specific network hubs that are disrupted by targeted therapy allows for the development of targeted interventions. Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS) and transcranial Direct Current Stimulation (tDCS) are being investigated as methods to "reactivate" weakened networks.[^40] For example, stimulating the dlPFC or the DMN hubs may help restore functional connectivity and improve cognitive performance in survivors. Cognitive rehabilitation programs that leverage the brain's neuroplasticity are also being refined using fMRI to monitor real-time functional recovery.[^40]

了解被靶向治疗破坏的特定网络中枢，可以支持靶向干预措施的开发。经颅磁刺激（TMS）和经颅直流电刺激（tDCS）正被研究作为“激活”减弱网络的方法。[^40] 例如，刺激 dlPFC 或 DMN 中枢可能有助于恢复功能连接并改善幸存者的认知表现。利用大脑神经可塑性的认知康复计划也正通过使用 fMRI 监测实时功能恢复来进行优化。[^40]

### The Impact of Novel Agents

### 新型药物的影响

As new targeted agents—including Antibody-Drug Conjugates (ADCs) and next-generation ICIs—enter the clinical arena, their neurotoxic profiles must be rigorously evaluated.[^42] Ongoing clinical trials, such as those utilizing the connectome-based clinical trial platform (CCTP), will be vital for mapping the brain's response to these novel therapies and ensuring that the pursuit of oncological efficacy does not come at an unacceptable cost to the patient's neurological health.[^43]

随着包括抗体偶联药物（ADCs）和下一代 ICIs 在内的新型靶向药物进入临床，必须严格评估它们的神经毒性特征。[^42] 正在进行的临床试验（如利用基于连接组的临床试验平台 CCTP 的试验）对于描绘大脑对这些新型疗法的反应至关重要，从而确保在追求肿瘤疗效的同时，不会给患者的神经健康带来无法接受的代价。[^43]

## Synthesis and Conclusion

## 总结与结论

The deep literature review conducted here reveals that the transition to targeted cancer therapies has not eliminated the risk of cognitive impairment but has instead introduced a new and complex set of neurobiological challenges. The use of Voxel-Based Morphometry and resting-state functional MRI has moved the field from subjective patient reports to an objective, systems-level understanding of the "survivor brain."

本文进行的深度文献综述揭示，向靶向癌症疗法的转变并未消除认知障碍的风险，反倒引入了一系列新的复杂神经生物学挑战。基于体素的形态学测量和静息态功能磁共振成像的使用，使该领域从主观的患者报告转向了对“幸存者大脑”客观的、系统级的理解。

Structurally, targeted and systemic therapies lead to widespread gray matter volume reductions, particularly within the frontal-temporal-insular axis.[^6] Functionally, these treatments disrupt the intrinsic connectivity of the Default Mode Network and reduce the global efficiency of the brain's communication networks.[^5] The convergence of these findings with the accelerated aging model provides a powerful framework for understanding CRCI as a form of treatment-induced biological aging.[^3]

在结构上，靶向和系统治疗导致广泛的灰质体积减少，特别是在额-颞-岛轴线内。[^6] 在功能上，这些治疗破坏了默认模式网络的内在连接，并降低了大脑通信网络的全局效率。[^5] 这些发现与加速衰老模型的趋同，为理解 CRCI 这种治疗诱导的生物学衰老形式提供了一个强大的框架。[^3]

For clinicians and researchers, the implications are clear: neuroimaging must be integrated into the long-term follow-up of cancer survivors. The identification of early structural and functional markers of neurotoxicity—such as insula atrophy or DMN decoupling—can facilitate early intervention through neuromodulation, cognitive rehabilitation, and the optimization of treatment regimens.[^40] As oncology moves toward a future of chronic disease management, protecting the "sanctuary" of the brain will be just as important as eradicating the primary tumor.

对于临床医生和研究人员来说，意义非常明确：神经影像学必须整合到癌症幸存者的长期随访中。识别神经毒性的早期结构和功能标志物（如岛叶萎缩或 DMN 解耦），可以通过神经调控、认知康复和治疗方案优化来促进早期干预。[^40] 随着肿瘤学向慢性病管理迈进，保护大脑这个“避风港”将与根除原发肿瘤同样重要。
