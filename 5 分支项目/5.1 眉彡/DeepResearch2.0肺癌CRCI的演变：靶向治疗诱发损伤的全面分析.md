# The Evolving Landscape of Neurocognitive Dysfunction in Lung Cancer: A Comprehensive Analysis of Targeted Therapy-Induced Impairment

# 肺癌相关认知功能障碍的演变：靶向治疗诱发损伤的全面分析

The shift toward precision oncology in the management of non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) and small cell lung cancer (SCLC) has revolutionized survival outcomes, yet it has simultaneously exposed a complex and often debilitating spectrum of neurocognitive morbidities. Cancer-related cognitive impairment (CRCI), traditionally associated with the cytotoxic fallout of systemic chemotherapy, is now recognized as a significant complication of targeted molecular therapies. As tyrosine kinase inhibitors (TKIs) targeting epidermal growth factor receptor (EGFR), anaplastic lymphoma kinase (ALK), and ROS1 increasingly penetrate the blood-brain barrier (BBB) to manage central nervous system (CNS) metastases, the unintended neurocognitive consequences have become a primary concern for clinical researchers and practitioners alike.[^1] This report provides an exhaustive review of the epidemiological, mechanistic, and clinical facets of lung cancer-related cognitive impairment, with a dedicated focus on the paradoxical neurotoxicity of high-penetrance targeted agents.

非小细胞肺癌（NSCLC）和小细胞肺癌（SCLC）管理向精准肿瘤学的转变彻底改变了生存结局，但同时也暴露了一系列复杂且往往致残的神经认知发病情况。癌症相关认知功能障碍（CRCI）传统上与全身化疗的细胞毒性副产物有关，但现在被认为是靶向分子治疗的一个重要并发症。随着针对表皮生长因子受体（EGFR）、间变性淋巴瘤激酶（ALK）和 ROS1 的酪氨酸激酶抑制剂（TKIs）越来越多地穿透血脑屏障（BBB）以管理中枢神经系统（CNS）转移，其意外的神经认知后果已成为临床研究人员和从业者的主要关注点[^1]。本报告详尽回顾了肺癌相关认知障碍的流行病学、机制和临床方面，并重点关注高渗透性靶向药物的矛盾神经毒性。

## Epidemiological Foundations of CRCI in Lung Cancer Populations

## 肺癌人群 CRCI 的流行病学基础

The prevalence of cognitive impairment in lung cancer is staggering, yet frequently under-reported in primary efficacy-driven clinical trials. Global estimates suggest that approximately 75% of patients experience measurable cognitive deficits during active treatment, with 35% suffering persistent impairment for years following the completion of therapy.[^1] In the context of lung cancer, specific prevalence rates vary significantly across regions and diagnostic cohorts, reflecting the interplay of biological susceptibility and clinical assessment rigor.

肺癌认知功能障碍的患病率令人震惊，但在以疗效为驱动的初步临床试验中往往被低估。全球估计显示，约 75% 的患者在积极治疗期间经历可衡量的认知缺陷，35% 的患者在治疗结束后多年仍遭受持续性损害[^1]。在肺癌背景下，具体的患病率在不同地区和诊断队列之间存在显著差异，反映了生物学易感性与临床评估严谨性之间的相互作用。

### Geographic and Assessment-Based Variability

### 地域与基于评估的变异性

A meta-analysis of lung cancer survivors revealed a pooled CRCI prevalence of 26%, though individual studies demonstrate a massive range from 6% to 84.4%.[^4] This heterogeneity is significantly influenced by the geographic region and the nature of the assessment. North American cohorts report a pooled prevalence of 44%, European studies report 33%, while Asian cohorts show a comparatively lower 15%.[^4] These discrepancies may arise from cultural differences in the reporting of subjective symptoms, varying access to intensive neuropsychological testing, or differences in treatment intensity and survivorship follow-up protocols.

一项针对肺癌幸存者的元分析显示，CRCI 的综合患病率为 26%，尽管个人研究显示的范围从 6% 到 84.4% 不等[^4]。这种异质性显著受到地理区域和评估性质的影响。北美队列报告的综合患病率为 44%，欧洲研究为 33%，而亚洲队列相对较低，为 15% [^4]。这些差异可能源于报告主观症状的文化差异、获得强化神经心理学测试的机会不同，或治疗强度和生存随访方案的差异。

### Baseline Vulnerability: Pre-treatment Cognitive Deficits

### 基线脆弱性：治疗前认知缺陷

A critical and often overlooked aspect of lung cancer is the presence of cognitive impairment prior to any therapeutic intervention. In patients with SCLC, approximately 31% exhibit cognitive dysfunction at diagnosis, particularly affecting executive functioning, verbal fluency, and processing speed.[^6] Structural MRI in these patients reveals baseline damage in the precuneus, fusiform-parahippocampal regions, and white matter tracts such as the cingulum and corpus callosum.[^6] This baseline vulnerability suggests that the malignancy itself, perhaps through paraneoplastic inflammatory cascades or systemic metabolic stress, primes the brain for further decline once therapeutic agents are introduced.

肺癌的一个关键且常被忽视的方面是，在任何治疗干预之前就已存在认知功能障碍。在 SCLC 患者中，约 31% 在诊断时即表现出认知功能障碍，特别是影响执行功能、语言流利度和处理速度[^6]。这些患者的结构 MRI 显示楔前叶、梭状回-海马旁回区域以及扣带束、胼胝体等白质束存在基线损伤[^6]。这种基线脆弱性表明，恶性肿瘤本身（可能通过副肿瘤性炎症级联反应或全身代谢压力）在大脑中埋下了隐患，一旦引入治疗药物，认知功能会进一步下降。

## Pathophysiological Mechanisms: Molecular and Cellular Drivers

## 病理生理机制：分子与细胞驱动因素

The etiology of CRCI in lung cancer is multifactorial, involving a convergence of systemic inflammation, oxidative stress, and the direct disruption of neural signaling pathways. Targeted therapies, while sparing many systemic tissues, often intersect with biological pathways essential for healthy brain function.

肺癌 CRCI 的病因是多因素的，涉及全身性炎症、氧化应激以及神经信号通路的直接破坏。靶向治疗虽然避开了许多全身组织，但往往与维持健康大脑功能必不可少的生物学通路发生交集。

### Cytokine Dysregulation and the Inflammatory Milieu

### 细胞因子失调与炎症环境

Malignancy and systemic therapies induce a proinflammatory state characterized by elevated levels of circulating cytokines. Proinflammatory markers such as IL−6, IL−12, and IL−1α have been directly linked to microglial activation and neuroinflammation.[^7] These cytokines can cross the BBB or stimulate the vagus nerve to transmit inflammatory signals to the CNS, where they interfere with synaptic plasticity and neuronal survival.[^7]

恶性肿瘤和全身治疗会诱导一种促炎状态，其特征是循环细胞因子水平升高。促炎标志物如 IL−6、IL−12 和 IL−1α 与小胶质细胞激活和神经炎症直接相关[^7]。这些细胞因子可以穿过 BBB 或刺激迷走神经，向 CNS 传递炎症信号，干扰突触可塑性和神经元存活[^7]。

Clinical evidence demonstrates a strong correlation between C-reactive protein (CRP) levels and cognitive dysfunction. In NSCLC patients receiving targeted therapy, CRP levels are significantly higher in those with cognitive disorders compared to those without.[^9] Furthermore, both CRP and psychological distress scores show an upward trend following the initiation of TKI therapy, suggesting that these agents may inadvertently exacerbate a state of chronic neuroinflammation.[^9]

临床证据表明，C反应蛋白（CRP）水平与认知功能障碍之间存在强相关性。在接受靶向治疗的 NSCLC 患者中，患有认知障碍者的 CRP 水平显著高于无认知障碍者[^9]。此外，CRP 和心理压力评分在开始 TKI 治疗后均呈上升趋势，表明这些药物可能会无意中加剧慢性神经炎症状态[^9]。

### Disruption of Hippocampal Neurogenesis and Neural Stem Cells

### 海马神经发生与神经干细胞的破坏

The hippocampus is a primary target of CRCI due to its reliance on continuous adult neurogenesis. The epidermal growth factor receptor (EGFR) pathway is a fundamental regulator of neural stem/progenitor cell (NSPC) proliferation, survival, and differentiation.[^10] Activation of EGFR signaling is sufficient to stimulate the entry of quiescent neural stem cells into the cell cycle, a process essential for memory formation and spatial navigation.[^10]

海马体是 CRCI 的主要靶标，因为它依赖于持续的成年神经发生。表皮生长因子受体（EGFR）通路是神经干/祖细胞（NSPC）增殖、存活和分化的基本调节因子[^10]。EGFR 信号的激活足以刺激静止的神经干细胞进入细胞周期，这一过程对 memory 形成和空间导航至关重要[^10]。

Potent, CNS-penetrant EGFR−TKIs such as osimertinib, while effectively inhibiting tumor growth, may concurrently suppress these vital signaling cascades in the subgranular zone of the hippocampus. Preclinical models suggest that mTOR, a downstream target of the PI3K/Akt pathway, is essential for EGFR-mediated NSPC proliferation.[^10] The inhibition of this axis leads to reduced neurogenesis, which manifests clinically as memory impairment and decreased executive control.[^10]

高效、具有 CNS 渗透性的 EGFR−TKIs（如奥希替尼）在有效抑制肿瘤生长的同时，可能同时抑制海马齿状回下区这些至关重要的信号级联反应。临床前模型表明，PI3K/Akt 通路的下游靶标 mTOR 对于 EGFR 介导的 NSPC 增殖至关重要[^10]。该轴的抑制会导致神经发生减少，在临床上表现为记忆受损和执行控制力下降[^10]。

## EGFR-Targeted Therapies and Neurocognitive Morbidity

## EGFR 靶向治疗与神经认知发病率

EGFR mutations are among the most common actionable drivers in NSCLC, occurring in 10−15% of Western populations and up to 40−50% of Asian populations.[^12] While first-generation (gefitinib, erlotinib) and second-generation (afatinib, dacomitinib) TKIs showed limited BBB penetration, the advent of third-generation osimertinib transformed the management of CNS disease.

EGFR 突变是 NSCLC 中最常见的可干预驱动因素之一，在西方人群中占 10−15%，在亚洲人群中高达 40−50% [^12]。虽然第一代（吉非替尼、厄洛替尼）和第二代（阿法替尼、达克替尼）TKIs 表现出有限的 BBB 渗透性，但第三代奥希替尼的出现改变了 CNS 疾病的管理。

### The Paradox of Osimertinib: Intracranial Control vs. Cognitive Cost

### 奥希替尼的悖论：颅内控制与认知代价

Osimertinib is a highly potent, CNS-active EGFR−TKI that has become the first-line standard of care.[^2] In trials like FLAURA and FLAURA2, osimertinib demonstrated superior PFS and CNS control compared to earlier TKIs.[^2] However, its high BBB penetration increases the brain's exposure to the drug.

奥希替尼是一种高效且具有 CNS 活性的 EGFR−TKI，已成为一线标准治疗方案[^2]。在 FLAURA 和 FLAURA2 等试验中，与早期 TKIs 相比，奥希替尼表现出优异的 PFS 和 CNS 控制能力[^2]。然而，其高 BBB 渗透性也增加了大脑接触药物的机会。

While generally well-tolerated, rare but severe neurocognitive disturbances have been documented with EGFR−TKIs. Case series of patients with advanced lung adenocarcinoma treated with EGFR−TKIs have reported symptoms such as sluggishness, memory deterioration, cognitive disorder, and even hallucinations.[^18] Brain MRI in these symptomatic patients showed increased ischemic foci, lacunar infarctions, worsening encephalatrophy, and white matter demyelination.[^18] These symptoms often proved irreversible despite the use of antipsychotic medications, highlighting the potential for permanent structural neurotoxicity.[^18]

虽然通常耐受良好，但已有记录显示 EGFR−TKIs 会引起罕见但严重的神经认知干扰。接受 EGFR−TKIs 治疗的晚期肺腺癌患者病例系列报告了诸如反应迟钝、记忆力减退、认知障碍甚至幻觉等症状[^18]。这些有症状患者的脑部 MRI 显示缺血灶增多、腔隙性脑梗死、脑萎缩加重和白质脱髓鞘[^18]。尽管使用了抗精神病药物，这些症状往往被证明是不可逆的，突显了永久性结构神经毒性的可能性[^18]。

## ALK and ROS1 Inhibitors: The Lorlatinib Challenge

## ALK 和 ROS1 抑制剂：洛拉替尼的挑战

Lorlatinib, a third-generation ALK/ROS1 inhibitor, represents the current frontier of CNS-penetrant targeted therapy. Designed to bypass P-glycoprotein-mediated efflux and penetrate the BBB, lorlatinib has achieved the longest reported PFS in NSCLC history.[^19] However, its safety profile is dominated by a unique spectrum of neurocognitive adverse events (NAEs).

洛拉替尼作为第三代 ALK/ROS1 抑制剂，代表了当前 CNS 渗透性靶向治疗的前沿。洛拉替尼旨在绕过 P-糖蛋白介导的外排并穿透 BBB，实现了 NSCLC 历史上报告的最长 PFS [^19]。然而，其安全性特征主要由一系列独特的神经认知不良事件（NAEs）所主导。

### Characterizing Lorlatinib-Induced NAEs

### 洛拉替尼诱导的 NAEs 特征

In the pivotal CROWN trial, lorlatinib demonstrated superior efficacy over crizotinib, but with a much higher incidence of NAEs (35−40% in clinical trials, up to 60% in some real-world cohorts).[^22] These events are categorized into four domains:

1. **Cognitive Effects:** Common symptoms include memory impairment, confusion, and "mental fogginess".[^22]
1. **Mood Effects:** These include anxiety, irritability, depression, and occasionally euphoria or affect lability.[^20]
1. **Speech Effects:** Patients may experience word-finding difficulties or changes in speech fluency.[^22]
1. **Psychotic Effects:** Hallucinations and paranoia represent the most severe end.[^22]

在关键的 CROWN 试验中，洛拉替尼表现出优于克唑替尼的疗效，但 NAEs 的发生率显著更高（临床试验中为 35−40%，在某些真实世界队列中高达 60%）[^22]。这些事件分为四个领域：

1. **认知影响：** 常见症状包括记忆受损、困惑和“大脑迷雾”[^22]。
1. **情绪影响：** 包括焦虑、易怒、抑郁，偶见欣快感或情感不稳定[^20]。
1. **言语影响：** 患者可能会经历找词困难或言语流畅性改变[^22]。
1. **精神病性影响：** 幻觉和偏执代表了最严重的程度[^22]。

### The Dopamine D2 Receptor Hypothesis

### 多巴胺 D2 受体假说

The precise mechanism of lorlatinib-induced NAEs remains under investigation, but research suggests an interaction between ALK inhibition and the dopaminergic system. ALK is known to regulate the internalization of the dopamine D2 receptor, a G protein-coupled receptor expressed in brain regions critical for motor function, motivation, and cognition.[^20] By inhibiting ALK in the healthy brain, lorlatinib may disrupt D2 receptor dynamics, leading to the observed cognitive and psychiatric symptoms.

洛拉替尼诱导的 NAEs 的确切机制仍在研究中，但研究表明 ALK 抑制与多巴胺系统之间存在相互作用。已知 ALK 调节多巴胺 D2 受体的内吞，该受体是在对运动功能、动机和认知至关重要的大脑区域表达的 G 蛋白偶联受体[^20]。通过抑制健康大脑中的 ALK，洛拉替尼可能会破坏 D2 受体的动力学，从而导致观察到的认知和精神症状。

## China-Specific Clinical Context and Expert Consensus

## 中国特定的临床背景与专家共识

In China, the high prevalence of EGFR and ALK mutations has led to extensive real-world experience with these agents. According to the **Chinese Expert Consensus on Clinical Management of Oncogene Addicted NSCLC with Leptomeningeal Metastasis (2026 Edition)**, high-CNS-penetration TKIs are the prioritized first-line intervention for patients with CNS involvement.

在中国，EGFR 和 ALK 突变的高患病率促使临床对这些药物积累了丰富的真实世界经验。根据 **《肺癌脑膜转移临床管理中国专家共识（2026版）》**，对于伴有 CNS 受累的患者，高 CNS 渗透性的 TKIs 被列为优先选择的一线干预措施。

The **Chinese Expert Consensus on Oncology Multimorbidity Management (2025 Edition)** defines "oncology multimorbidity" (肿瘤共病) as one or more complications or other disease states that coexist with malignancy and impact its diagnosis, treatment, and prognosis. This consensus advocates for a "Tumor-Comorbidity" integrated management mindset, emphasizing that cognitive impairment should be monitored every cycle during active treatment and every 3 months during follow-up. Furthermore, for elderly patients (≥65 years), standardized geriatric assessments (GA) are recommended to balance efficacy with quality of life, identifying cognitive vulnerabilities not captured in routine exams.

**《中国肿瘤共病防治专家共识（2025版）》** 将“肿瘤共病”定义为与恶性肿瘤并存、对肿瘤诊治和预后产生影响的一种或多种并发症或其他疾病状态。该共识提倡“肿瘤-共病”整体管理思维，强调在积极治疗期间每个周期、在随访期间每3个月都应监测认知功能。此外，对于老年患者（≥65 岁），建议进行标准化的老年综合评估（GA），以平衡疗效与生活质量，识别常规检查无法捕捉的认知脆弱性。

## Clinical Risk Factors and Vulnerability Stratification

## 临床风险因素与脆弱性分层

Identifying high-risk individuals is paramount for personalized management. Advanced age remains the most significant predictor of severe NAEs, with patients ≥65 years showing a 2.6-fold higher hazard.[^24] Beyond biological factors, social support has emerged as a critical mitigative element. Patients living alone or lacking a supportive partner are significantly more likely to require hospitalization for neurocognitive crises.[^27]

识别高风险人群对于个性化管理至关重要。高龄仍是严重 NAEs 最显著的预测因子，≥65 岁患者的风险高出 2.6 倍[^24]。除生物学因素外，社会支持已成为一种关键的缓解因素。独居或缺乏支持性伴侣的患者因神经认知危机而需要住院治疗的可能性显著更高[^27]。

## Management and Rehabilitation Strategies

## 管理与康复策略

Addressing CRCI requires a multimodal approach involving pharmacological adjustments, physical activity, and technology-based rehabilitation.

- **Dose Modification:** In the CROWN study, NAEs were managed through dose interruption (62%) and reduction (23%). Post-hoc analyses confirm that dose modification does not compromise systemic or intracranial efficacy.
- **Pulmonary and Physical Rehabilitation:** Multidisciplinary programs focusing on aerobic exercise and breathing techniques have shown improvements in HRQoL and functional capacity.
- **Digital Cognitive Rehabilitation:** AI-driven applications and computerized training address memory and attention deficits through individualized adaptation.

解决 CRCI 需要多模态方法，包括药物调整、体育活动和基于技术的康复。

- **剂量调整：** 在 CROWN 研究中，NAEs 通过剂量中断（62%）和减量（23%）来管理。事后分析确认，剂量调整不会损害全身或颅内疗效。
- **肺部与体能康复：** 侧重于有氧运动和呼吸技术的跨学科项目已显示出在生活质量（HRQoL）和功能能力方面的改善。
- **数字认知康复：** AI 驱动的应用程序和计算机化训练通过个性化适应来解决记忆和注意力缺陷。

## Conclusion

## 结论

Lung cancer-related cognitive impairment has emerged as a central challenge in the age of precision oncology. While third-generation TKIs offer unprecedented survival benefits, their high BBB penetration necessitates a proactive approach to neurocognitive safety. The 2025-2026 clinical landscape, characterized by treatment intensification and the integration of Chinese expert consensus, emphasizes early screening, personalized dose strategies, and comprehensive rehabilitation to ensure that patients not only live longer but do so with their cognitive independence intact.

肺癌相关认知障碍已成为精准肿瘤学时代的核心挑战。虽然第三代 TKIs 提供了前所未有的生存益处，但其高 BBB 渗透性要求采取主动的神经认知安全措施。以治疗强化和中国专家共识整合为特征的 2025-2026 临床图景，强调了早期筛查、个性化剂量策略和全面康复，以确保患者不仅能活得更久，而且能在保持认知独立的情况下生活。
