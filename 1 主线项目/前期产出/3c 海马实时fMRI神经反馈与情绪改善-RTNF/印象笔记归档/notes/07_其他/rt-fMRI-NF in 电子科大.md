people：Benjamin Becker，Kendrick, 姚树霞, Zhiying Zhao

  


Voluntary control of anterior insula and its functional connections is feedback-independent and increases pain empathy, 2019, NeuroImage

The therapeutic potential of rtfMRI-NF training depends critically upon whether: (1) acquired self-regulation produces functionally relevant changes at behavioral and brain network levels and (2) training effects can be maintained in the absence of feedback. 

To address these key questions, the present study combined rtfMRI-NF training for acquiring volitional anterior insula (AI) regulation with a sham-controlled between-subject design.

实验fMRI localizer task (7min)：左侧前岛叶（LAI）功能定位，给被试看图示他人疼痛状态的图/中性图片（blocked design) ，指导被试想象图中的人有多痛

  * 图片来自 Meng et al. (2012) (mean valence 2.90 ± 1.15, mean arousal 5.07 ± 1.03)，中性图片为书、椅子之类 (mean valence 4.95 ± 0.15, mean arousal 2.77 ± 0.44)
  * blocked design: 8 blocks (4 blocks of neutral pictures and 4 blocks of painful pictures) alternating with a 30 s baseline interval (fixation cross).Each picture was presented for 2 s and there were 10 pictures within each block.

  
NF training task:

  * adopted from a procedure used by Caria et al. (2010)
  * 4组练习，每组10分钟；每组：5个调节block+6个基线block, 每个block 30秒
  * 每个block: 展示LAI区内活动，每2s更新 
    * 调节block: 箭头要求向上调整，指导被试回忆、想象个人事件中的负面情绪 (Berman et al., 2013, Caria et al., 2007, Caria et al., 2010)
    * 基线block要求向下调整，指导被试睁眼休息，把AI活动降至基线
    * 控制组为假反馈：ROI为远离LAI层面的全脑活动
  * 测量AI调控功能：每个block后跟一张疼痛图片（8s）+2个评价task（每个6秒，评估pain empathy+arousal)

  
Resting state task: 每次扫三个rest（闭眼）  
Follow-up assessment：

  * 训练2日后
  * 不显示LAI活动，仅要求根据训练时的经验进行活动调整
  * 练习2组

Zhao Z, Yao S, Li K, Sindermann C, Zhou F, Zhao W, Li J, Lührs M, Goebel R, Kendrick KM, Becker B. Real-Time Functional Connectivity-Informed Neurofeedback of Amygdala-Frontal Pathways Reduces Anxiety. Psychother Psychosom. 2019;88(1):5-15. doi: 10.1159/000496057. Epub 2019 Jan 30. PMID: 30699438.

Methods: In a randomized crossover sham-controlled design, 26 healthy subjects with high anxiety underwent real-time fMRI-guided neurofeedback training to enhance connectivity between the ventrolateral prefrontal cortex (vlPFC) and the amygdala (target pathway) during threat exposure. Maintenance of regulatory control was assessed after 3 days and in the absence of feedback. Training-induced changes in functional connectivity of the target pathway and anxiety ratings served as primary outcomes.
