comment：

  1. 当时的影响力比较大，上了editorial, CME course, AJP Audio, Video。目前引用144

  2. We previously showed rtfMRI-nf training to be feasible in enhancing amygdala response to positive memories in healthy (25) and depressed individuals (9). 有两个前期实验

     1. depressed individuals (9)，具体研究：Young KD, Zotev V, Phillips R, et al.: Real-time fMRI neurofeedback training of amygdala activity in patients with major depressive disorder. PLoS One 2014 

     2. in healthy (25)，具体研究：Zotev V, Krueger F, Phillips R, et al.: Self-regulation of amygdala activation using real-time fMRI neurofeedback. PLoS One 2011;

  3. 本研究设计：

     1. 样本：36 unmedicated adult MDD 

     2. 基线：明确sample中杏仁核激活程度确实是升高了  amygdala activity is hypoactive in our depressed sample

     3. rtfMRI-nf session：determine whether additional sessions are useful

     4. 1-week 随访: 明确疗效 to examine changes in clinical symptoms and determine whether changes persist beyond the neuro feedback sessions.

     5. Autobiographical Memory Test：to determine whether amygdala rtfMRI-nf training can also improve memory recall in depressed participants, as impaired recall for specifific positive memories constitutes an enduring cognitive defificit in depression (26, 27) that is unaltered by current treatments (28).

  4. Procedure: 4 visits

     1. visit 1 基线量表+自传体记忆: 完成量表 BDI-II，Snaith-Hamilton Pleasure Scale (SHAPS)，MADRS，HAMD-21，HAMA， Autobiographical Memory Test

     2. visit 2, 时间间隔 5-7d，第一次 rt-fMRI-nf，完成自评量表（同v1）

     3. visit 3，间隔1w，内容同v2

     4. Visit 4, 间隔5-7d，内容=rt-fMRI-nf+v1，包括自传体集体测试

     5. ![[1 主线项目/前期产出/3c 海马实时fMRI神经反馈与情绪改善-RTNF/印象笔记归档/attachments/Bodurka 杏仁核抗抑郁系列研究_files/].png]]

     2. 调节策略：检索积极回忆（retrieve positive memories)

后续研究2：前后杏仁核task及静息态fc改变

  1. Altered task-based and resting-state amygdala functional connectivity following real-time fMRI amygdala neurofeedback training in major depressive disorder， Neuroimage clinical. 2017 Dec 5;17:691-703.

  2. 主要结果：Results: Following training, amygdala connectivity during positive AM recall increased with widespread regions in the frontal and limbic network. During rest, amygdala connectivity increased following training within the fronto-temporal-limbic network. During both task and resting-state analyses, amygdala-temporal pole connectivity decreased. We identified increased amygdala-precuneus and amygdala-inferior frontal gyrus connectivity during positive memory recall and increased amygdala-precuneus and amygdala-thalamus connectivity during rest as functional connectivity changes that explained significant variance in symptom improvement. Amygdala-precuneus connectivity changes also explain a significant amount of variance in neurofeedback regulation success.
